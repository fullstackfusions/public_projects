## Use Case: Real-Time AI Insights — Transforming Client Conversations into Tailored Financial Solutions

Recruiting new clients involves digesting financial information from bank statements, conversations, etc. (i.e., unstructured data). This takes the advisor (and their team) a significant amount of time.

**Step 1:** Leverage AI to accept a prospect's inputs to build out a Proposal/Financial Plan, that can be validated by an Advisor and then reviewed by the client within the same meeting, with the subsequent opportunity to tweak the proposal. AI would be able to tap into the product/model research material to determine risk and alignment to client's risk tolerance.

**Step 2:** Upon acceptance of the Proposal online:

- (i) The Account Open process for the client would be automatically triggered off, both on the custodian platform (Broadridge) as well as on Client Source, and downstream systems, eliminating the need for manual work.
- (ii) Corresponding IPS data would be populated on the relevant systems requiring it, e.g., IPS, pre/post trade compliance, etc.

**Step 3:** Once funds have been transferred in, based on the plan and IPS, trigger off a rebalancing of the portfolio. IA would review recommendations before submitting orders and continue on with the lifecycle of Investment Management thereof.

**Benefits:**

- Quicker time to start managing (setup) a client's investments
- Time liberation for Advisors
- Creation of preliminary financial plans and/or asset mix generation for clients based on responses to questionnaire
- Advisor help and insights
